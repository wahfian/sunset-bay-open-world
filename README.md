Sunset Bay is an original top-down open-world crime game: explore a sun-soaked bay city on foot and by car, steal cars, run five story missions for fixer Dana Cole, dodge the police as your wanted level rises, and fight, deliver, and escape your way to the Sunset Heist.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Play with WASD to move and drive, E to steal a car, F to shoot, Space to punch, Shift to run, P to pause, M to mute. Progress saves in your browser.
